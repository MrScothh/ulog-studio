# Code of conduct

ULog Studio is a small project for people who fly, build and tune drones. To keep it a good place to ask questions and contribute:

- **Be respectful and patient.** People arrive with very different experience; questions about basics are welcome.
- **Discuss the work, not the person.** Disagree with arguments, data and logs, not with insults.
- **Respect privacy.** Do not post other people's logs, positions or personal details. Ask before sharing anything you received privately.
- **No harassment**, discrimination, sexualised content or personal attacks, in any space of the project (issues, pull requests, discussions, wiki).
- **Safety first.** Do not encourage flying that endangers people or breaks local rules.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and block participants who do not follow these rules. Report problems to the maintainer through a [private security advisory](https://github.com/MrScothh/ulog-studio/security/advisories/new) or by contacting the maintainer on GitHub.

Inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
